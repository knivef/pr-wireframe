What is a self-hosted crypto payment gateway? ▲
A self-hosted crypto payment gateway is software you install and run on your own server, giving you full control over your payment infrastructure without relying on a third-party custodian or intermediary.

PayRam accepts USDT, USDC, and other major stablecoins across Ethereum, Polygon, Base, and Tron. Payments settle directly to your wallet with no intermediary holding your funds.
