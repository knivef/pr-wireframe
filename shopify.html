The Problem
Shopify merchants are stuck with custodial crypto plugins
✕
Shopify merchants have no native way to accept crypto without using a custodial third-party processor that holds their funds for days before settling.
✕
Existing Shopify crypto plugins route through custodians, charge high conversion fees, and often trigger Shopify's 2% third-party transaction fee on top.
✕
Merchants selling to crypto-native customers lose sales because their checkout has no stablecoin option — forcing buyers to convert to fiat first.
How It Works
Live in four steps. No developer needed.
1
Install from App Store
Find PayRam in the Shopify App Store, click install, and authorize it on your store.
2
Connect your wallet
Enter your self-hosted PayRam wallet address. This is where all payments settle.
3
Configure currencies
Choose USDC, USDT, or both, and select which networks to accept on.
4
Go live
PayRam appears at checkout. Customers pay. Funds settle to your wallet instantly.

App Store
Listed in the Shopify App Store
The PayRam plugin appears in the Shopify App Store like any other payment provider. Click Install, authorize the app on your store, and the setup wizard guides you through the remaining configuration in under 5 minutes.

Configuration
Plugin settings panel
Enter your PayRam wallet address, toggle which stablecoins to accept (USDC, USDT, or both), and select which blockchain networks to enable. All settings update in real time — no page rebuild required.
Plugin config screen — wallet input, currency toggles, network selector
Customer view
