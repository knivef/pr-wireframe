Funds collected. Automatically swept. Always in your wallet.

SmartSweep monitors all customer deposit wallets and automatically consolidates incoming funds into your master wallet on a schedule or threshold you define.

Automated consolidation Threshold-based Gas-optimized
Animated diagram — multiple customer wallets draining into one master wallet
The Problem
Fragmented balances are an operational liability
✕
Platforms with many customer deposit wallets accumulate fragmented balances spread across dozens or hundreds of addresses — impossible to manage manually.
✕
Manually sweeping funds from each address is operationally expensive and error-prone — especially when multiple chains are involved.
✕
Gas costs vary by network and timing, making manual sweeps financially wasteful without optimization logic.
How It Works
Set rules once. SmartSweep does the rest.
1
Set your sweep rules
Define threshold (e.g. sweep when >$50) and schedule in the Operator dashboard.
2
SmartSweep monitors
PayRam watches every customer deposit address for incoming funds in real time.
3
Trigger fires
Threshold or schedule triggers — SmartSweep bundles sweep transactions for gas efficiency.
4
Funds in master wallet
Consolidated funds arrive in your designated master wallet. Gas is optimized.

Automated treasury. Zero manual sweeps.

Deep Dive
Configure, monitor, and optimize
Configuration
Sweep rules panel
Set a balance threshold (e.g. trigger sweep when wallet balance exceeds $50), choose a schedule (every 6h, daily, or custom), and enter your master wallet address. All via the Operator dashboard — no code required.
Sweep rules panel — threshold, schedule, master wallet inputs
Sweep History
Complete activity log
Every SmartSweep event is logged in the Operator dashboard with source wallets, total amount swept, network fee paid, and the resulting master wallet transaction hash. Fully exportable for accounting.
Sweep activity log — amount, source wallets, gas cost, timestamp
Gas optimization
Batching vs. individual sweeps
SmartSweep groups multiple small wallet sweeps into a single batched transaction when economical, dramatically reducing total gas spend compared to sweeping each wallet individually.
Diagram — batched sweep vs. individual sweeps gas comparison
Use Cases
Who uses SmartSweep
Exchange Operator
Consolidates user deposit balances hourly to keep master wallet funded for withdrawals. Zero manual intervention — all automatic via scheduled sweeps.
Marketplace
Sweeps seller escrow wallets into treasury daily without manual ops involvement. SmartSweep handles the timing and gas optimization automatically.
SaaS Platform
Automatically collects subscription payments from customer wallets into a single operating account — no manual treasury management required.
