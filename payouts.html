The Problem
Paying people globally is broken
✕
Paying multiple recipients in crypto requires manual transaction signing, address management, and reconciliation — especially at volume.
✕
Traditional wire and ACH payouts take 1-5 business days and carry high fees for international recipients — unacceptable for real-time platforms.
✕
Building a payout system in-house requires significant infrastructure, compliance overhead, and ongoing maintenance to stay reliable at scale.
How It Works

1

4
Settled on-chain
Funds leave your master wallet and arrive in recipient wallets within seconds.

Deep Dive
Every way to send a payout
Single payout
Simple dashboard form
Enter a recipient wallet address, amount, currency, and optional memo. Submit. The payout fires immediately and appears in your history with a live status. No code required for ad-hoc payouts.
Single payout form — address, amount, currency, memo
Batch payouts
Upload a CSV. Send hundreds at once.
Import a CSV with recipient addresses, amounts, and currencies. PayRam validates the list, shows a preview with total, and executes the batch in one operation. Ideal for weekly seller payouts or contractor runs.
Batch CSV upload — recipient list with status preview
Payout history
Complete audit trail
Every outgoing payout is logged with recipient label, wallet address, amount, chain, status (pending / confirmed / failed), and txn hash. Filter by date range, recipient, or status. Export to CSV for accounting.
Payout history table — status, recipient, amount, txn hash
