The Problem
Crypto-only checkout costs you customers
✕
Most crypto-native merchants lose customers who don't have a wallet or don't understand how to send crypto to a checkout address.
✕
Existing fiat-to-crypto onramps are slow, expensive, and require users to complete KYC before they can transact.
✕
Merchants accepting only crypto are limiting their addressable market to crypto-native users — a fraction of potential buyers.
How It Works
Card payment. Crypto settlement.
1
Customer selects card
Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or SEPA at checkout.
2
PayRam processes card
Onramp partner handles the fiat leg securely and compliantly.
3
Conversion happens
Fiat converts to merchant's preferred stablecoin at market rate.
4
Merchant receives crypto
USDC or USDT lands directly in your self-hosted wallet.

Deep Dive
The full flow, visualised
Customer view
Familiar checkout, zero friction
The customer sees a standard card entry form with Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, and Google Pay logos. They complete payment exactly as they would on any e-commerce site — no wallet address, no crypto knowledge required.
Card checkout UI — Visa / Mastercard / Apple Pay / Google Pay logos
Conversion flow
Fiat in. Stablecoin out.
PayRam routes the fiat transaction through an onramp partner who handles FX and compliance. The resulting stablecoin (USDC or USDT) is sent directly to the merchant's configured wallet address on the selected chain.
Fiat → onramp partner → stablecoin → merchant wallet diagram
Operator dashboard
Card-originated settlements labelled separately
Incoming settlements from the onramp are labelled as card-originated in your Operator dashboard so you can distinguish them from direct crypto payments. Full breakdown by method, amount, and chain.

Use Cases
Who uses Card-to-Crypto
E-commerce Merchant
Sells globally and wants to accept cards without giving up crypto settlement or dealing with a traditional PSP's reserves and delays.
SaaS Founder
Charges subscription fees in USDC but wants non-crypto customers to subscribe using their card. Bridge the gap without running two separate billing systems.
Gaming / iGaming Platform
Accepts deposits via card from players who don't hold crypto, converts to stablecoin for in-platform use — no wallet required for the player.
